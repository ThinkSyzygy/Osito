# PROJECT-ORION charter

> **Fictional example:** all content below is invented.

## Purpose

Create a small enclosure prototype that lets a desktop sensor circuit exchange room air while remaining stable on a desk and accessible for bench service.

## Scope

Included:

- enclosure geometry for a nonregulated demonstration circuit;
- passive air openings;
- access to the rear cover;
- small-batch additive prototype parts;
- documented design and validation evidence.

Excluded:

- electronics or sensor-algorithm design;
- environmental sealing;
- certification;
- production tooling;
- use for safety, health, or building control.

## Success criteria

- Accepted requirements have linked verification evidence.
- No blocking design-review findings remain open.
- The final fictional prototype configuration and residual risks are documented.
