# Passive inlet clearance

> **Fictional example:** all dimensions, methods, and outcomes are invented.

## Requirement

When the enclosure rests on a flat surface, every intended air inlet shall retain at least 2.5 mm of unobstructed normal clearance from that surface.

## Rationale

The initial concept uses passive side inlets. A minimum geometric clearance makes the requirement inspectable without claiming a sensor-performance result.

## Acceptance

Inspect the final assembled prototype on a flat reference plate at four orientations. Record the minimum inlet-to-plate clearance and photographs or sketches of the setup.

## Verification

The first observation exposed a marginal rear inlet condition. [ECN-ORION-001](../changes/ECN-ORION-001.md) increased the rear foot height, and [VAL-ORION-001](../validation/VAL-ORION-001.md) records the fictional follow-up pass.
