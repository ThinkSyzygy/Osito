# Bench service access

> **Fictional example:** the requirement and result are invented.

## Requirement

The rear cover shall be removable and reinstallable five consecutive times using one common hand-tool type without cracking the enclosure or leaving a loose fastener.

## Rationale

The demonstration circuit needs occasional bench access during software development.

## Acceptance

One trained reviewer performs five complete remove/install cycles on the final prototype. The result records damage, retained hardware, and any loss of engagement.

## Verification

[VAL-ORION-001](../validation/VAL-ORION-001.md) records the fictional pass. This is a bench-service check, not a life-cycle or product-safety qualification.
