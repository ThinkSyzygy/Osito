# Desktop envelope

> **Fictional example:** the values and evidence are invented.

## Requirement

The assembled enclosure shall fit within an external rectangular envelope of 112 mm by 82 mm by 42 mm.

## Rationale

The demonstration unit must fit the allocated area on a generic desk shelf.

## Acceptance

Inspection of the final prototype at 23 °C shall show every external point inside the stated envelope. A calibrated measurement method and the observed maximum dimensions must be recorded.
