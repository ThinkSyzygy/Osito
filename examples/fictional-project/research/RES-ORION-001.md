# Qualitative comparison of prototype inlet locations

> **Fictional example:** the experiment, observations, and conclusion are invented and are not real performance data.

## Question

Which inlet location is easiest to inspect and least directly obstructed by a flat support surface in the demonstration enclosure?

## Scope

Three simple foam-board mockups represented underside, low-rear, and side inlets. This activity compared visibility and direct geometric obstruction only. It did not measure flow rate, sensor response, contamination, acoustics, or thermal behavior.

## Observations

- The underside opening was hidden during placement and directly faced the support surface.
- The low-rear opening was visible from one angle but depended on foot height.
- The two side openings remained visible in all four mockup orientations.

## Finding

Side openings were the least directly obstructed option in this limited comparison. The result supports concept selection but does not predict airflow or sensor performance.

## Recommendation and uncertainty

Use side openings for the first prototype and retain a measurable clearance requirement for the low rear edge. A qualified test would still be required for any response-time or accuracy claim.
