# Turn placement assumptions into geometric acceptance criteria

> **Fictional example:** the lesson and supporting evidence are invented.

## Observation

The first concept assumed that feet would keep a low inlet clear, but no measurable criterion existed until the placement risk was reviewed.

## Evidence and cause

The fictional revision-A observation found 1.6 mm clearance. The confirmed contributor was the specified rear support height relative to the inlet edge. No claim is made about sensor performance or airflow.

## Reusable principle

When product placement can obstruct a functional opening, define an inspectable geometric boundary before interpreting performance data.

## Applicability limits

The principle does not select an appropriate clearance value, predict flow, or replace environmental testing. Those depend on the actual product, physics, use case, and qualified engineering judgment.

## System change

Future enclosure charters should ask whether placement affects functional openings and, if so, link a measurable requirement and validation method.
