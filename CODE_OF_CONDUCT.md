# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Osito contributors and maintainers commit to a professional, inclusive, and technically honest community. Participation should be safe for people of different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, disciplines, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Ask clarifying questions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Give actionable, proportionate review feedback.
- Credit contributions and sources.
- Respect confidentiality, privacy, licensing, and project boundaries.
- Correct mistakes openly and report failed checks honestly.
- Consider the safety consequences of technical guidance.
- Help newcomers understand the reasoning behind repository conventions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or unwelcome personal attention
- Publishing another person's private information
- Sharing client, employer, supplier, or proprietary material without authorization
- Deliberately introducing credentials, malicious prompts, unsafe automation, or deceptive examples
- Misrepresenting test results, engineering evidence, authorship, licensing, or review status
- Sustained disruption of technical discussion or retaliating against good-faith reports

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, reviews, project communication, and public spaces where someone represents Osito.

## Enforcement

Use an existing private maintainer channel for conduct concerns when one is available; otherwise, do not disclose sensitive conduct details in a public issue. For security or privacy exposures, use [Security](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove content, request corrective action, restrict participation, or ban participants based on severity and pattern.

Enforcement decisions should protect affected people, minimize unnecessary disclosure, and avoid conflicts of interest. Do not publish sensitive conduct details. Use an existing private maintainer channel when one is available; the project does not claim a dedicated reporting channel unless one is actually configured and monitored.
